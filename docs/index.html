01問題The problem

車、四足機器人、無人機、手持裝置的運動方式差很多。比賽要求同一組權重通吃，且不告訴模型是哪一種，只給 IMU 訊號、要它輸出速度。難處是加速度累加成速度時，誤差會越積越大。

A car, a quadruped robot, a drone and a handheld rig all move differently. The challenge asks for one set of weights across all four, without telling the model which one it is: it gets only the IMU signal and must output velocity. It is hard because the error grows as acceleration is accumulated into velocity.

03做法Approach

核心是一個很小的網路，把訊號切成一秒一段、連著讀，輸出速度。本專案的技術貢獻：

The core is one small network: it cuts the signal into one-second windows, reads them in sequence and outputs velocity. Project contributions:

  • 發現模型不理會空氣阻力：把加速度故意放大或縮小，預測幾乎不變。於是照物理造出更多快飛資料。
  • 先校正、再接力：先校正感測器，再讓速度在段與段之間接力。
  • 時間縮放增強：把飛行紀錄快轉。
  • 評估方法：一般的驗證資料看不到最難的快飛，所以另外準備兩組考題。改動要大過只換種子的起伏才算數。
  • Finding that the model ignores air drag: when the acceleration was deliberately scaled up or down, the prediction barely moved, so more fast-flight data was generated from physics.
  • Calibrate first, then carry over: calibrate the sensor first, then carry the velocity over from one window to the next.
  • Time-scaling augmentation: fast-forward the flight recordings.
  • Evaluation method: the standard validation data never shows the hardest fast flights, so two extra test sets were built, and a change only counts if it exceeds the seed-to-seed swing.

限制：前兩項進步不大，和只換種子的起伏同一量級，不能算確定有效。第三項不是新點子，效果也和同時拉長的預訓練分不開。Limit: the first two gains are small, about the size of the seed-to-seed swing, so they cannot be counted as certainly effective. The third is not a new idea, and its effect cannot be separated from the longer pre-training introduced at the same time.

限制與細節Limits and details
  • 四條衝刺序列都在公開榜那一半（測試資料的另一半），所以第一項對私榜名次沒有貢獻。
  • 「接力力度」是取捨，開小有益，開大會讓一般行駛變遲鈍。
  • 時間縮放這一類做法，至少有四隊各自獨立用過。
  • 訓練前會先做自監督預訓練：遮住一部分輸入再還原，不需要速度答案。
  • 數字見 results.md。
  • All four sprint sequences are in the public half of the leaderboard (the other half of the test data), so the first item did not help the private rank.
  • The carry-over strength is a trade-off: a little helps, a lot makes ordinary driving sluggish.
  • At least four other teams independently used the same family of time-scaling augmentation.
  • Before training, the network is pre-trained with a self-supervised task: hide part of the input and reconstruct it, which needs no velocity labels.
  • Numbers: results.md.

04賽後延伸Post-contest extension

比賽結束後我繼續改進。這部分不計名次，也不和比賽成績連成「進步」。After the contest I kept improving the model. This part does not count for rank, and it should not be read as a continuation of the contest score.

  • 借來的：另一支隊伍 thisray 賽後公開了後處理方法（模型輸出後，再用物理規則修正）。我把它接到我的模型上，這是階段一，也是效果的主要來源。
  • 我加的：階段二，針對地面機器人把修正調成一半強度；階段三，同一段資料從幾個角度各預測一次，預測差越多越沒把握，平滑時就少相信（這個點子是我提的）。
  • 怎麼把關：我加的兩步都先留一批資料當考題，並在看結果前寫下怎樣才算有效。限制：階段二的強度是看過這批資料才選的，還沒在新資料上驗證。
  • Borrowed: another team, thisray, released a post-processing method after the challenge (it fixes the model's output afterwards with physics rules). I ran it on my model. That is Step 1, and the main source of the gain.
  • Added by me: Step 2 applies the ground-robot correction at half strength. In Step 3 the same window is predicted from several angles, and the more the predictions disagree, the less the smoother trusts that window (my idea).
  • How I checked: for both of my steps I set aside a batch of data as an exam and wrote down what would count as success before looking at results. Caveat: the strength in Step 2 was chosen after seeing that data and has not been checked on new data.

借來的方法對無人機很有效，對車子反而變差。我加的兩步各只再進步一點點。

The borrowed method works very well on drones and makes cars worse; my two steps each add only a little more.

限制與細節Limits and details
  • thisray 的全名是 Ssu-Rui Lee。程式 Apache-2.0，權重 CC-BY-NC-4.0（限研究用途）。
  • 階段一也先寫了判準，並在無人機錄製上做過留出檢查。但車、四足機器人、手持裝置當時沒有乾淨的正面證據，把關比階段二、三弱。
  • 階段一分幾次補交逐步接上。最後兩版裡，後一版私榜略差、公開榜較好，我兩版都上傳後留下後一版（圖上就是它）。
  • 階段二的一半強度是看過留出資料才選的，三次檢驗用同一批，不能說已在新場次證明有效。
  • 在留出的地面錄製上，階段二比完整後處理好約 6%（三次檢驗：原本的權重、同種子重訓一次、換種子重訓一次，結果一致）。
  • 完整後處理讓無人機的速度誤差約少一半，車子的約增加 15%。
  • 我的階段一和 thisray 公布的成績（取自其報告，我未重驗）差距，小於只換種子的起伏，不能說誰較好。
  • 完整表格與算法見 post_competition.md。
  • thisray's full name is Ssu-Rui Lee; the code is Apache-2.0 and the weights are CC-BY-NC-4.0 (research use only).
  • Step 1 also had its pass/fail rule written first and a held-out check on drone recordings, but for the car, the quadruped and the handheld rig there was no clean positive evidence at the time, so its checking is weaker than for Steps 2 and 3.
  • Step 1 was added over several late submissions. Of the last two variants, the later one scored slightly worse on private and better on public; I uploaded both and kept the later one (the one in the chart).
  • The half strength in Step 2 was chosen after seeing the held-out data, and all three checks reuse that set, so it is not shown to hold on new sessions.
  • On the held-out ground recordings, Step 2 is about 6% better than the full post-processing (three checks: the original weights, one retraining with the same seed and one with a different seed, all consistent).
  • The full post-processing cuts the drone's velocity error by about half and raises the car's by about 15%.
  • The gap between my Step 1 and the score thisray reports (from their report, not re-verified) is smaller than the swing from the random seed, so I cannot say which is better.
  • Full table and method: post_competition.md.

06我的角色My role

我是研究負責人：研究方向、取捨、驗證規則與每次提交與否，由我最後決定。實作、實驗執行、程式撰寫與互審由 AI coding agent（Claude Code、Codex）完成。我把「規劃並指揮多個 agent 的研究流程、防止 AI 自己驗證自己」當成一項能力：留出考題資料、上傳前先寫判準、由另一個模型重算、專門挑錯的審查。

I was research lead: research direction, trade-offs, validation rules and whether each submission went out were my final decisions. Implementation, experiment execution, coding and cross-review were done by AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex). I treat planning and directing a multi-agent research workflow while preventing AI self-verification as a skill: a held-out set, rules written before submission, recomputation by a different model, and adversarial review.

明確是我提出的點子：針對不同載具做不同物理建模（無人機用動力學）；把每個窗的不確定度接進平滑器（就是階段三）；選定試多對齊平均（結果無效）；賽後繼續推分；以及賽後各步驟哪些值得提交的取捨。其餘方法標為「本專案」。

Ideas that were explicitly mine: platform-specific physics models (dynamics for drones); feeding per-window uncertainty into the smoother (which became Step 3); choosing to try multi-alignment averaging (which did not work); continuing to push after the contest; and the call on which post-contest steps were worth submitting. Everything else is credited to "this project".
