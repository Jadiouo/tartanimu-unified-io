01問題The problem

車、四足狗、無人機、手持裝置，各自的運動方式完全不同。比賽要求同一組權重處理這四種載具，推論時不知道是哪一種，只給 200 Hz 的六軸 IMU（三軸加速度加三軸角速度），輸出速度。難在：IMU 量到的是加速度，積分起來會越漂越遠；而每種載具的動力學不一樣，例如無人機高速時空氣阻力很大。

A car, a quadruped, a drone and a handheld rig all move differently. The challenge asks for one set of weights across all four, with no platform label at inference: only 200 Hz six-axis IMU data (3-axis accelerometer and 3-axis gyro) in, velocity out. It is hard because the IMU measures acceleration, which drifts when integrated, and each platform has different dynamics; a fast drone, for example, is dominated by air drag.

03做法Approach

一個 2.17M 參數的網路：把資料切成 1 秒的窗，用雙向循環網路一次看 40 個窗；前面接兩個小模組先校正感測器偏差與姿態，後面是讓速度在窗與窗之間接力的輸出，接力的力度可以調。訓練前先讓模型玩「遮住一部分輸入再還原」的自監督預訓練。以下是本專案的技術貢獻：

One 2.17M-parameter network: the data is cut into 1-second windows and a bidirectional recurrent network reads 40 windows at once; two small modules in front correct sensor bias and attitude, and an output behind lets the velocity estimate carry over from window to window, with an adjustable strength. Before training it is pre-trained on a "mask part of the input and reconstruct it" task. Project contributions:

  • 診斷「模型不讀空氣阻力」。把無人機衝刺序列的加速度放大或縮小，預測幾乎不動（變動小於 0.3 m/s），所以用符合物理的縮放造出更多快飛資料。官方分數 0.2702 → 0.26577，差距小於只換種子的飄動；四條衝刺序列都在 Kaggle 公開榜那一半，對私榜名次沒有貢獻。
  • 先校正、再接力。官方分數 0.26577 → 0.25878；同時量出「接力力度」是取捨：開小有益，開大會讓一般行駛變遲鈍。
  • 時間縮放增強（把紀錄快轉）。0.2837 → 0.2687，但同時把預訓練從 90 分鐘延長到 3 小時，兩者效應分不開；同類做法至少 4 隊獨立用過，不是新穎點。
  • 評估方法：用三把尺量三種不同的資料。以「只換種子就會飄動的幅度」當門檻，擋掉太小的改動。
  • Diagnosing that the model ignores drag. Scaling a drone sprint's acceleration up or down barely moves the prediction (less than 0.3 m/s), so I built more fast-flight data with physics-consistent scaling. Official score 0.2702 to 0.26577, smaller than run-to-run variation from random seeds; all four sprint sequences sit in the public half of the leaderboard, so this did not help the private rank.
  • Correct first, then carry over. Official score 0.26577 to 0.25878; I also measured that the carry-over strength is a trade-off: small helps, large makes ordinary driving sluggish.
  • Time-scaling augmentation (fast-forwarding the recordings). 0.2837 to 0.2687, but pre-training was lengthened from 90 min to 3 h at the same time, so the effects cannot be separated; at least 4 other teams used the same family of augmentation, so it is not novel.
  • Evaluation method: three rulers for three kinds of data. Run-to-run variation from random seeds is the gate that rejects changes that are too small.

04賽後延伸Post-contest extension

賽後提交不計名次，也不與上面的比賽成績並列成「進步」。算法不同：比賽成績是私榜（48 條序列）的 0.19912；下圖是賽後補交、Kaggle 回傳的分數。Late submissions do not count for rank and are not a "progress line" from the contest score. Different basis: the contest score is the private half (48 sequences) 0.19912; the chart below shows Kaggle-returned scores for post-contest late submissions.

先借用另一支參賽隊伍 thisray（Ssu-Rui Lee）賽後公開的後處理方法（程式 Apache-2.0；權重 CC-BY-NC-4.0），接到我的網路上，這是階段一（E）；再由本專案加兩步：階段二（F），地面機器人的修正只套一半強度；階段三（G），把「同一段資料從幾個角度預測、預測彼此不一致的程度」當作每個窗的不確定度，接進平滑器（這個點子是我提的）。階段二、三各自先拿出一部分資料不讓模型看、當作考題檢驗，並在上傳前先寫下怎樣才算有效；借來的階段一沒有這層保證。三個階段在 Kaggle 公開榜與私榜（測試資料的兩半）的分數見下圖，換算成官方分數約 0.182、0.179、0.178（近似，誤差約 ±0.0005）。

First I ported the post-processing method that another team, thisray (Ssu-Rui Lee), released after the challenge (code Apache-2.0, weights CC-BY-NC-4.0) onto my network, giving Step 1. This project then added two steps: Step 2, a half-strength correction for ground robots, and Step 3, where how much several predictions of the same window disagree is used as that window's uncertainty and fed into the smoother (my idea). Steps 2 and 3 were each checked on a held-out set the model never saw, with the pass/fail rule written before submission; the borrowed Step 1 has no such guarantee. Scores on the Kaggle public and private halves of the test data are charted below; converted to the official score they are about 0.182, 0.179, 0.178 (approximate, error about ±0.0005).

  • 地面機器人的修正，相對完整的後處理：分數 −5.74%、−6.12%、−5.67%（三次訓練，27 條地面留出錄製，95% 區間排除 0；越低越好）。
  • 完整的後處理對無人機留出錄製的速度誤差 −49.0%，對車子的速度誤差 +14.6%：他人的修正對無人機很有效，對地面載具幾乎沒有淨效果。
  • 接上同一個公開方法時，我的階段一約 0.182，thisray 公布 0.188（其報告，我未重驗），差距小於只換種子的飄動，不能說誰較好。
  • Ground-robot correction vs the full post-processing (score): −5.74%, −6.12%, −5.67% (three trainings, 27 held-out ground recordings, 95% intervals exclude 0; lower is better).
  • The full post-processing cuts velocity error on the drone held-out set by 49.0% but raises it on cars by 14.6%: the borrowed correction is very effective on drones and nets almost nothing on ground platforms.
  • With the same published method, my Step 1 is about 0.182 and thisray reports 0.188 (their report, not re-verified). The gap is smaller than run-to-run variation from random seeds; neither is better.

06我的角色My role

我是研究負責人：研究方向、取捨、驗證規則與每次提交與否，由我最後決定。實作、實驗執行、程式撰寫與互審由 AI coding agent（Claude Code、Codex）完成。我把「規劃並指揮多個 agent 的研究流程、防止 AI 自己驗證自己」當成一項能力：留出考題資料、上傳前先寫判準、由另一個模型重算、專門挑錯的審查。

I was research lead: research direction, trade-offs, validation rules and whether each submission went out were my final decisions. Implementation, experiment execution, coding and cross-review were done by AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex). I treat planning and directing a multi-agent research workflow while preventing AI self-verification as a skill: a held-out set, rules written before submission, recomputation by a different model, and adversarial review.

明確是我提出的點子：針對不同載具做不同物理建模（無人機用動力學）；把每個窗的不確定度接進平滑器（就是階段三）；選定試多對齊平均（結果無效）；賽後繼續推分；以及賽後各步驟哪些值得提交的取捨。其餘方法標為「本專案」。

Ideas that were explicitly mine: platform-specific physics models (dynamics for drones); feeding per-window uncertainty into the smoother (which became Step 3); choosing to try multi-alignment averaging (which did not work); continuing to push after the contest; and the call on which post-contest steps were worth submitting. Everything else is credited to "this project".
